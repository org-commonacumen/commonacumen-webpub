    The ABCs of Empowering Builders to combine Human and Token capital to achieve more

    Labor Day is a week away, so this is a shout out to the people - the Labor Force, the Builders. AI transformation is not just about Large Language Models (LLMs) and adopting tools. It is about empowering more people to become Builders — people who can combine human capital with token capital to redesign how work gets done.

    This September at Microsoft DevDays Asia 2026, I’ll be expanding last year’s 5As framework — AI, Agents, Apps, Analytics, and All Data — into a broader People, Process, and Technology model: the ABCs of Agency, Builders, and Capabilities.

    I have had the privilege to work at Microsoft for nearly 20 years, assisting customers to realize Microsoft’s Mission to “Empower every person and organization on the planet to achieve more”. How do you use the ABCs to Amplify the Agency and Ambition of the people around you and realize the vision of achieving more?

    The ABC Framework: Agency, Builders, Capabilities

    Agency = choice, autonomy, ambition, ability to act

    Builders = people who create workflows, apps, agents, analytics, and improved processes

    Capabilities = both human capabilities and technical capabilities

    For me the ABCs framework includes the combination of Agency, Builders, and Capabilities - and the journey takes Discovery, Experience, and a Frontier Mindset.

    The ABC framework is inspired by Self-determination Theory (SDT) that postulates that individuals are naturally driven to grow and learn when their 3 basic psychological needs are met. Those three needs are Autonomy, Competency, and Relatedness.

    Drilling into Agency, I will start from the technology perspective of the 5As: AI, Agents, Analytics, Apps, and All Data. When I say All Data I mean both the structured and unstructured data available inside and outside the organization that individuals and organizations can use to amplify their Agency and ambition.

    Focusing on people, they must be empowered through the use of these 5A technologies so they are provided the choice of platforms and tools that enable people to become Builders; not just consumers, but Builders of solutions and better business processes.

    It is the combination of Agency, and Builders and their Capabilities that move organizations to becoming Frontier firms. The Builders leverage the 5As along with the uniquely Human Capabilities that Ryan Roslansky and Aneesh Raman refer to in their book Open to Work as the 5Cs: Curiosity, Creativity, Communication, Courage, and Compassion.

    From 5As to ABCs: Technology foundation meets people/process/technology

    Builders are the ones in the organization that drive realization of the People - Process - Technology triangle.

    Processes like Plan → Build → Run

    Who are Builders?

    Builders are not limited to professional developers. They include researchers, business users, knowledge workers, creatives, and IT professionals – anyone who turns expertise into reusable capability.

    Builders and Use Case:

    • A sales leader building a customer-insight agent
    • A finance analyst creating a Fabric app
    • A developer using GitHub Copilot CLI to automate delivery
    • A business user turning recurring work into an agentic workflow

    The role of Microsoft platforms and tools:

    Microsoft has many platforms and tools that place AI in the flow of human ambition. M365 Copilot with its Chat, Cowork, and to be released Code, and Autopilots (like Scout) is a discovery and execution canvas for everyone. These Copilot AI tools also show up in Office and Teams and when using these tools all that data is captured in the Microsoft Graph where Builders can use unstructured data to build Agents in Copilot Studio, Foundry, and GitHub Copilot and combine it with structured data using Fabric Data Agents. Microsoft platforms and tools help build a virtuous cycle that captures knowledge and compounds intelligence. A continuous learning loop that combines Human capital + Token capital build frontier firms.

    So what are the ABCs of learning, building, and reaching frontier? It takes a framework and a journey, but it really takes Builders.

    GitHub Copilot CLI makes anyone a Builder.

    Bring together all data with Microsoft IQ.

    Bringing the ABCs together - Human capital + token capital: How intelligence compounds

    And just like the overlaps you will find in the 5As (where it is difficult to discern where technology is categorized because AI, Agents, Apps, Analytic, and All Data are part of all things in GenAI) there are other listings of Cs like:

    Satya Nadella’s The Reverse Information Paradox: Control, Capability, Choice, Cost, Compound.

    Organizations need to leverage Human Capital plus Token Capital to become a Frontier Firm. Frontier Firms own their AI learning loop — building proprietary intelligence on top of foundation models using Reinforcement Learning Environments (RLEs) and Private Evaluations, rather than renting static AI Capabilities from a vendor.

    Token capital is the reusable AI-powered work product an organization accumulates: prompts, agents, evaluations, workflows, embeddings, and institutional knowledge encoded into systems.

    Human Capital + Token Capital = Frontier Firm

    Microsoft’s consultative mindset of: Context, Curiosity, and Co-create related to conversations which are part of communication.

    Context which is so critical for working with Large Language Models (LLMs). In terms of a consultative mindset Context is how you prepare for a customer conversation grounded in the customer’s reality, including their industry, stakeholders, and the key decisions.

    Curiosity is present in Open to Work’s 5Cs as well. Curiosity is how we slow down, understand what really matters, and move conversations to the right problem. It is also how we keep continuously learning throughout our lives.

    Then there is Co-creation where a person and an LLM can create a new App, or work in teams and with customers. Co-create is where understanding turns into direction, and value takes shape

    Whether it is the Open to Work 5Cs or Satya’s The Reverse Information Paradox 5Cs or The Consultative Mindset 3Cs they are not steps; they’re part of the transformation and conversation. The real skill is sensing which one the transformation and conversation needs now — and responding with intention instead of habit.

    To quote Open to Work, “Don’t just be open to work; be open to building the next version of work.”
