    The ABCs of Empowering Builders to combine Human and Token capital to achieve more

    I am presenting a couple of sessions later this month at Microsoft DevDays Asia 2026. Here is some of the strategy and thinking behind expanding the AI, Agents, Apps, Analytics, and All Data approach that I referred to as the 5As last year. This year I am expanding it to be a composite of People, Processes and Technology. The expansion of the approach for this year is titled, “The ABCs of Empowering Builders to combine Human and Token capital to achieve more”. For individuals trying to get their organization to move forward to become a Frontier Firm, hopefully this post can help articulate the need and provide motivation to migrate, modernize, and transform the way you and others work in your organization. This will all take people, process, and technology to learn the ABCs of AI transformation.

    I have had the privelage to work at Microsoft for nearly 20 years, helping empower customers to realize Microsoft’s Mission to “Empower every person and organization on the planet to achieve more”. From the strategy and thought leadership perspective what are the ABCs of realizing this vision of achieving more as an individual person and for a broader organization? How do you Amplify the Agency and Ambition for you and the people around you?

    Microsoft has many platforms and tools that place AI in the flow of human ambition. M365 Copilot with its Chat, Cowork, and to be released Code, and Autopilots (like Scout) is a discovery and execution canvas for everyone. These Copilot AI tools also show up in Office and Teams and when using these tools all that data is captured in the Microsoft Graph where Builders can then build Agents in Copilot Studio, Foundry, and GitHub Copilot and Fabric (structured data). Microsoft platforms and tools help build a virtuous cycle that captures knowledge and compounds intelligence. A continuous learning loop that combines Human capital + Token capital build frontier firms.

    So what are the ABCs or learning, building, and reaching frontier? It takes a framework and a journey.

    ABC Framework

    For me the ABCs framework includes the combination of Agency, Builders, and Capabilities.

    The journey takes Discovery, Experience, and a Frontier perspective and mindset.

    The ABC framework is inspired by Self-determination Theory (SDT) that postulates that individuals are naturally driven to grow and learn when their 3 basic psychological needs are met. Those three needs are Autonomy, Competency, and Relatedness.

    Drilling into Agency (Choice) I will start from the technology perspective of the 5As: AI, Agents, Analytics, Apps, and All Data. When I say All Data I mean both the structured and unstructured data available inside and outside the organization that individuals and organisations can use to amplify their Agency and ambition.

    Focusing on people, they must be empowered through the use of these 5A technologies so they are provided the choice of platforms and tools that enable people to become Builders; not just consumers, but Builders and consumers of solutions and better business processes.

    Who are Builders

    Builders are the ones in the organization that drive realization of the People - Process - Technology triangle.

    Processes like Plan → Build → Run

    It is the combination of Agency, and Builders and their Capabilities that move organizations to becoming Frontier firms. The Builders leverage the 5As along with the uniquelly Human Capabilities that Ryan Roslansky and Aneesh Raman refer to in their book Open to Work as the 5Cs: Curiosity, Creativity, Communication, Courage, and Compassion.

    And just like the overlaps you will find in the 5As (where it is difficult to discern where technology is categorized because AI, Agent, Apps, Analytic, and All Data is part of all things in GenAI) there are other listings of Cs like: 1) Satya Nadella’s The Reverse Information Paradox: Control, Capability, Choice, Cost, Compound.

    Organizations need to leverage Human Capital plus Token Capital to become a Frontier Firm. Frontier Firms own their AI learning loop — building proprietary intelligence on top of foundation models using Reinforcement Learning Environments (RLEs) and Private Evaluations, rather than renting static AI Capabilities from a vendor.

    Human Capital + Token Capital = Frontier Firm

    2) Microsoft’s consultative mindset of: Context, Curiousity, and Co-create related to conversations which are part of communiction.

    Context which is so critical for working with Large Language Models (LLMs). It terms of a consultative mindset Context is how you prepare for a customer conversation grounded in the customer’s reality, including their industry, stakeholders, and the key decisions.

    Curiosity is present in Open to Work’s 5Cs as well. Curiosity is how we slow down, understand what really matters, and move conversations to the right problem. It is also how we keep continuously learning throughout our lives.

    Then there is Co-creation where a person and an LLM can create a new App, or work in teams and with customers. Co-create is where understanding turns into direction, and value takes shape

    Bringing the Cs together

    The Cs aren’t steps; they’re a conversation choreography. You already know Context, Curiosity, and Co-create. The real skill is sensing which one the conversation needs now — and responding with intention instead of habit.

    To quote Open to Work, “Don’t just be open to work; be open to building the next version of work.”
